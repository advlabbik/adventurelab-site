# AdventureLab, the media company specialized in bicycle tourism

## We operate in the bicycle tourism market all across Europe

Since 2012, when we decided to bring the concept of **bikepacking** to Italy, our mission has been to delve into the realms of reading passions, intercepting trends, and anticipating the times. In these 11 years, we created the first real organization of **cycling events** dedicated to adventure.

Our events represent the best that sport and adventure can offer and are distinguished by our firm opposition to competitive fanaticism. For this reason, we firmly promote the concept of “no race” in all our initiatives.

Today, we are market leaders, **with events organized all across Europe.** Bright as the North Star, our course is charted and the destination decided. Accompany us on this journey. We will guarantee you an exciting journey.

A true hotbed of ideas and inspiration where sport, the beauty of the world, and adventure are the true protagonists.

## **Gravel**

We are pioneers and top gravel experts in Italy and beyond, with over a decade of experience in the **Tuscany Trail**, the world’s premier bikepacking/gravel event with nearly 5,000 registered riders each year.

Thanks to our “**Unpaved Roads**” circuit of events with stages throughout Italy, we have a following of **thousands of enthusiasts.**

Our in-depth knowledge of the needs of gravel bike riders allows us to offer unique experiences, combining skills in thematic mapping and territorial enhancement.

Bicycle touring has been changing its appearance to bikepacking for years now. Since 2012, we have introduced and developed the bikepacking phenomenon in Italy with Bikepacking.it

Specializing in creating multi-day itineraries for all types of bikes, we have more than a decade of experience.

Thanks to our experience we are able to create itineraries of 4,500 km across the European continent perfect for biking.

## **Mountain Bike**

The advent of e-MTB has forever changed the face of mountain biking and, as a result, the face of bicycle touring by opening up a new world of possibilities. Now off-road cycling has become accessible to a much wider and growing audience.

Our expertise in creating dedicated MTB areas, from mapping to related services, makes us experts in enhancing the area for MTB enthusiasts of all levels.

In the beginning, our vision of using the racing bicycle not for competition but as a means for unique experiences, such as exploring new territories, was poorly understood. Fortunately, today this concept is widely accepted.

We develop routes exclusively on asphalt, away from the chaos of traffic, dedicated to cyclists who want to make the most of their racing bikes. We create routes throughout Europe, favoring bike paths and roads less traveled, ensuring a safe and immersive cycling experience.

## Promotion of the territory :

We’re committed to promoting the regions hosting our events as **bike-friendly destinations**.

By spotlighting local routes and facilities that cater to cyclists, we aim to showcase these areas as top choices for biking enthusiasts.

Our events not only highlight the natural beauty and cultural richness but also emphasize the accessibility and support that these communities offer to those on two wheels, **making them ideal for bike touring**.

## Promotion of local culture :

Our events are not just about the ride; they celebrate local culture. As we cross each region, we shine a light on the **unique traditions and heritage of the places we visit**.

We aim to enrich the cycling experience by integrating the cultural tapestry of the host territories, offering participants a ride that’s as culturally rewarding as it is physically challenging.

## Promoting food and wine culture :

At the heart of our events lies a journey through the rich culinary landscapes of our host regions. **We celebrate local gastronomy** by integrating regional delicacies and traditional beverages into the experience.

It’s an opportunity for participants to savor the authentic tastes that define each territory, creating a flavorful ride that indulges all the senses.

This is our way of promoting and honoring the **unique enogastronomic identity of every place our wheels touch**.

## We organize sports events in your region

In these **11 years** of activity, as event organizers, we have involved **tens of thousands of people** who have generated hundreds of thousands of overnight stays of cycle tourists. Beyond the finish lines and geographic destinations, the true heartbeat of our events is the sharing of a deeper ideal.

It’s an invitation to rediscover the freedom of two wheels, to connect with nature, and to forge unbreakable bonds with fellow enthusiasts. With every pedal stroke and trail explored, it’s not just the body that moves, but the soul, too.

Our vision transcends mere competition; it’s a movement that celebrates the pure essence of sport. The Adventure as a form of knowledge and personal growth. Together, we create not just events but unforgettable experiences that leave a lasting imprint on the memories and hearts of all participants.
